# VIX gate study, 2026-10-06 (RQ7)

Question: does gating NEW ENTRIES on a VIX-derived risk-off signal improve the deployed
v2 fleet (5 trend bots off non-core capital + 50% SPY core) out of sample?

Data: CBOE VIX and VIX3M daily closes (free CBOE history), forward-filled onto the fleet's
SIP trading dates 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-06; gate uses the close of day i, the same timing
as the bots' own signals. Engine: research/common.py, identical settings to
`python3 v2/fleet.py backtest`. Script: research/rq7_vix_gate.py. Entry-only gates: open
positions keep their stops and trend exits.

Adoption rule (same as the rest of this project): Sharpe AND MAR must improve in BOTH the
BACK-OOS window (2016-01 to 2020-08, never tuned on) and the TUNE window, and the result
must sit on a plateau under +-20% of its parameter, not a lone spike.

## Verdict: NOT adopted

- **No gate passes the rule.** Absolute levels (VIX < 20/25/30/35), 1-year percentiles,
  and term structure (VIX/VIX3M contango) are all flat to worse out of sample. VIX < 20
  blocks 30% of days and cuts CAGR from 17.3% to 11.4%: it mostly keeps the fleet out of
  the strong rebounds (2020: 6% vs 33%). Backwardation gates block the same days.
- **The closest candidate is "VIX below its own 10 to 30 day average"** (fear falling).
  It forms a real plateau in the FULL and TUNE windows (Sharpe 0.98 to 1.05 vs 0.94,
  maxDD 25 to 28% vs 30%, 17% fewer trades) but in the untuned 2016 to 2020 window it is
  flat (Sharpe 0.64 to 0.71 vs 0.68). SMA10 alone technically clears the rule by +0.03
  Sharpe; its neighbours do not, which is the lone-spike pattern the rule exists to reject.
- **Why it does not help OOS:** the fleet's trend gate (price above a rising EMA20 with
  ADX above 25, plus the SPY confirm) already refuses to enter during the high-VIX
  drawdowns. A VIX gate blocks the first days of the recovery instead, which on 3x ETFs
  are the most valuable days of the year. The 2018 and 2022 losses are essentially
  unchanged under every gate because they come from positions opened before the spike.
- **Cost of being wrong:** none for now; this is a no-change decision.

## Recommendation

Leave v2 as deployed. Re-run this script once the fleet has 12 months of live decisions:
if the "VIX below its 20 to 30 day average" plateau still holds in sample and the live
record shows entries into rising-fear tape losing money, it becomes a defensible change.
Until then it is a 2016-2020-neutral, 2020-2026-positive result, which is exactly what a
fitted filter looks like.
